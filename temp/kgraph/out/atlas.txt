ATLAS — climb from the base up to the summit

SUMMIT: Become capable of building a small language model from scratch in Rust — no ML frameworks, implemented myself all the way down to the tensor math. The target is a compact transformer-based next-token language model, trained on a small text corpus on a single machine, capable enough to form basic grammatical sentences: greet people, complete simple sentences, and do light grammar correction. I want to understand every layer well enough to implement it in Rust myself: text tokenization (a simple byte-pair-encoding or character/word vocabulary), token and positional embeddings, the transformer decoder block (self-attention, multi-head attention, feed-forward layers, layer normalization, residual connections), the language-modeling head with cross-entropy loss, reverse-mode automatic differentiation / backpropagation and the gradients of these operations, a training loop with an optimizer (SGD / Adam), and autoregressive text generation with sampling (greedy, temperature, top-k). Small / educational scale only on modest data — explicitly NOT large-scale or distributed training, GPU kernel programming, pretraining at scale, or RLHF / fine-tuning.
