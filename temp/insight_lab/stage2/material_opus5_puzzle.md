# [opus5] MATERIAL — Equally Common, Unequally Awaited  ·  form: **puzzle**

_Why this form:_ It poses as a crisp question with objective numeric answers (6 vs 4) that are reachable by pure head-arithmetic, and the tempting wrong answer ('same, by symmetry') is exactly where the delight lives. The mechanism — self-overlap and clumping — is the payoff, not the calculation.
